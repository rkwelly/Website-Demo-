Why it takes three days

Slow fermentation isn't a marketing line here — it's the actual schedule the dough is on before it ever sees an oven.

Day 1, evening

Mix and autolyse

Flour and water rest together for an hour before the starter and salt go in, so the gluten develops on its own first.

Day 2, overnight

Cold retard

Shaped loaves go into the walk-in at 3°C for 18 hours. This is where most of the flavour actually comes from.

Day 3, 5am

Bake

Straight from cold into a 260°C stone oven, steam-injected for the first ten minutes to get the crust we're known for.
